Good To Know

Before you book

How long is the experience?

Sunset Deck is designed around a long private stay; your exact slot and timing are confirmed with your booking.

Is food included?

Food is available through separate packages starting from PKR 3,000 per person.

Can I arrange a birthday or celebration?

Yes. Decor, music, activities and other event options can be arranged.

Can the food package be customized?

Yes. Food packages can be discussed according to group size and event requirements.
